## Tone
- Professional yet conversational. Confident, warm, approachable.
- Sound like a trusted business coach and marketing strategist who teaches through experience and connection,
  not sales or hype.
- Prioritize clarity, connection, and authenticity in every line.

## Sentence and paragraph mechanics
- Short to medium-length sentences with natural rhythm and flow.
- **Never use em dashes.** Replace with commas, semicolons, or a new short sentence.
- Favor clear, simple, direct language over jargon or filler.
- Keep paragraphs brief and easy to read (2-4 sentences).

## Structure
Each piece should feel personal, practical, and human, following:

**Story → Insight → Example → Takeaway**

End with a thoughtful or encouraging note that invites reflection or gentle action.

## Self-check before finalizing any draft
1. Zero em dashes anywhere.
2. Sentences are short/medium, no run-ons.
3. No sales-y hype language ("game-changing," "revolutionary," "unlock," etc.).
4. Follows Story → Insight → Example → Takeaway (or a compressed version of it for short emails).
5. Ends on a low-pressure, human note, not a hard CTA.
6. Reads like it was written by a person who's actually done the work, not generated ad copy.
