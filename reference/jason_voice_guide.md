# Jason Nuss Voice Guide

Source: extracted from "Jason Nuss Writing Style / Voice Application Prompt for ChatGPT.docx" (the voice-application prompt; the full standalone "Jason Nuss Writing Voice Guide" document it references was not present in the repo — this file is the authoritative voice reference until that document is added).

## Tone
- Professional yet conversational. Confident, warm, approachable.
- Sounds like a trusted business coach and marketing strategist who teaches through experience and connection, not sales or hype.
- Never pushy. Never salesy.

## Sentence and paragraph mechanics
- Short to medium-length sentences with natural rhythm and flow.
- Never use em dashes. Replace with commas, semicolons, or short separate sentences.
- Clear, simple, direct language. No jargon, no filler.
- Keep paragraphs brief and easy to read (1-3 sentences typical).

## Structure
Each piece should feel personal, practical, and human, following:
**Story → Insight → Example → Takeaway**

## Closing
End with a thoughtful or encouraging note that invites reflection or gentle action. Low pressure, never a hard close.

## Overall priority
Clarity, connection, and authenticity in every line, above all else.

## Self-check checklist (apply before finalizing any email)
- [ ] Zero em dashes anywhere in the copy
- [ ] Sentences are short to medium, no run-ons
- [ ] No jargon or generic filler language
- [ ] Tone reads as warm/coach-like, not salesy or hype-driven
- [ ] Follows the sequence's intended structure (see mailer brief)
- [ ] Ends with a low-pressure, reflective or gentle-action close
- [ ] References the contact's first name and at least one real, specific researched detail (not generic praise)
