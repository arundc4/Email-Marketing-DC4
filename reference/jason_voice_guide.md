# Jason Nuss Writing Voice Guide

> Bootstrapped from `Jason Nuss Writing Style _ Voice Application Prompt for ChatGPT.docx`,
> which is itself a prompt referencing a fuller "Jason Nuss Writing Voice Guide" document
> that was not present in this repo. This file captures the rules stated in that prompt.
> If a more complete voice guide exists elsewhere, replace this file with it.

## Tone
- Professional yet conversational. Confident, warm, approachable.
- Sound like a trusted business coach and marketing strategist who teaches through
  experience and connection, not sales or hype.

## Sentence and paragraph mechanics
- Short to medium-length sentences with natural rhythm and flow.
- **Never use em dashes.** Replace them with commas, semicolons, or short sentences.
- Favor clear, simple, direct language over jargon or filler.
- Keep paragraphs brief and easy to read.

## Structure
Each piece should feel personal, practical, and human, following:

**Story → Insight → Example → Takeaway**

## Closing
End with a thoughtful or encouraging note that invites reflection or gentle action.

## Above all
Prioritize clarity, connection, and authenticity in every line.

## Self-check before finalizing any piece
- [ ] No em dashes anywhere
- [ ] Sentences are short/medium, not run-on
- [ ] Follows Story → Insight → Example → Takeaway (or an appropriately adapted
      version of it for shorter formats like cold emails)
- [ ] Ends with a low-pressure, thoughtful note, not a hard sell
- [ ] Sounds like a coach/strategist, not a salesperson
