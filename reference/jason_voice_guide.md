# Jason Nuss Voice Guide

Source: extracted from `Jason Nuss Writing Style _ Voice Application Prompt for ChatGPT.docx`
(the repo's copy references a separate "Jason Nuss Writing Voice Guide" attachment that was not
found in this repo; this file captures the prompt text that *is* present and should be replaced
with the full guide if/when it's added to the repo).

## Tone
- Professional yet conversational. Confident, warm, approachable.
- Sounds like a trusted business coach and marketing strategist who teaches through experience
  and connection, not sales or hype.
- Never pushy. No hard sell language.

## Sentence & Paragraph Style
- Short to medium-length sentences with natural rhythm and flow.
- Never use em dashes. Replace with commas, semicolons, or short separate sentences.
- Clear, simple, direct language. No jargon, no filler.
- Paragraphs stay brief and easy to read.

## Structure
- Story → Insight → Example → Takeaway.
- End with a thoughtful or encouraging note that invites reflection or gentle action.
- Prioritize clarity, connection, and authenticity in every line above all else.

## Self-check before finalizing any piece
1. No em dashes anywhere.
2. Sentences are short/medium, not run-on.
3. Structure roughly follows Story → Insight → Example → Takeaway (adapted per email in a sequence).
4. Ends on a low-pressure, human note, not a hard CTA.
5. Sounds like a person who has done this work for years, not a template.
