## Tone
- Professional yet conversational: confident, warm, approachable.
- Sounds like a trusted business coach and marketing strategist who teaches through
  experience and connection, not sales or hype.
- Prioritize clarity, connection, and authenticity in every line.

## Mechanics
- Short to medium-length sentences with natural rhythm and flow.
- Never use em dashes. Replace them with commas, semicolons, or short sentences.
- Favor clear, simple, direct language over jargon or filler.
- Keep paragraphs brief and easy to read.

## Structure
Story → Insight → Example → Takeaway.

End with a thoughtful or encouraging note that invites reflection or gentle action.

## Self-check before finalizing any piece
- [ ] No em dashes anywhere.
- [ ] Sentences are short to medium, not run-on.
- [ ] Reads as warm/direct, not hypey or salesy.
- [ ] Follows Story → Insight → Example → Takeaway where applicable.
- [ ] Ends with a low-pressure, thoughtful close.
