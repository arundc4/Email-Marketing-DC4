# DC4 (Digital C4) Brand Notes

Source: digitalc4.com homepage + about-us page (research, Aug 2026).

## What DC4 Sells
B2B digital marketing services focused on building pipeline and driving
growth for marketing teams, not vanity metrics like traffic.

## Core Service Lines
1. Content Programs - structured content aligned to visibility and revenue.
2. SEO/AEO - SEO combined with AI-search (answer engine) visibility.
3. Paid Media - Google, LinkedIn, Meta campaign management.
4. Marketing Automation - HubSpot implementation and infrastructure.
5. White Label Services - outsourced execution for other agencies.

## Target Clients
Marketing directors, VPs, demand gen managers, and CMOs at mid-sized to
enterprise B2B companies (often SaaS, technology, education) who want
expert-level channel execution without adding headcount.

## Positioning / Core Narrative
"Activity doesn't build pipeline. We help B2B marketing teams build the
programs that do." Emphasis on the gap between marketing activity (traffic,
posts, campaigns) and actual qualified pipeline.

"Digital C4 delivers more than marketing. We deliver momentum."

## Differentiators
- Founders (Jason Nuss, Chuck Swanson) stay hands-on through the engagement,
  not just at kickoff.
- Partnership model, not vendor model. Works as an extension of the internal
  team without adding headcount.
- Data-driven, transparent, tied to pipeline/revenue outcomes.
- Question mapping and answer-first content architecture for AI visibility.
- Hybrid onshore/offshore delivery model for efficiency.
- Founded 2010, 15+ years in B2B digital marketing.

## Brand Voice
Direct and strategic. Confident without boasting. Precise, outcomes-oriented
language. Positions DC4 as a partner who understands the mechanics of B2B
growth, not a tactical vendor checking boxes.

## How to Reference DC4 in Emails (Email 3 "soft example")
Keep it light: one sentence naming the relevant service line (content, SEO/AEO,
paid media, or marketing automation) and tying it to the specific challenge
identified for that company. Never oversell. No case-study fabrication;
only reference DC4's real service lines and philosophy above.
