## What DC4 sells
B2B digital marketing agency services:
- Content programs (structured content aligned to visibility, authority, and revenue, not just traffic)
- SEO / AEO (traditional search ranking plus AI-generated-answer visibility, an "Answer-First Content Framework")
- Paid media management across Google, LinkedIn, and Meta, focused on audience quality and conversion intent
- Marketing automation (infrastructure connecting channels, capturing leads correctly)
- White-label services for agencies

## Who they sell to
B2B marketing teams and leaders trying to build pipeline and revenue through structured, repeatable marketing
programs, not one-off campaigns.

## Core positioning / tagline
**"Activity Doesn't Build Pipeline."**
The core wedge: most B2B marketing generates traffic, content, and activity that never converts into real
pipeline. DC4 positions itself as the fix, building the actual programs (not just tactics) that convert.

## Tone
Direct, strategic, confidence-driven. Consultative, not salesy. Speaks with authority about marketing
problems while staying focused on measurable business outcomes.

## Differentiators to draw on in outreach
- Distinguishes "activity" (busywork marketing output) from "pipeline" (revenue-connected outcomes) as the
  central diagnostic lens for prospect problems.
- Treats SEO and AEO as one connected discipline (ranking for humans and being cited by AI answer engines).
- Frames paid media around lead quality and intent, not just spend or impressions.
- Frames automation as infrastructure work: connecting channels and capturing leads correctly, not just
  "email drip campaigns."

## How to use this in the mailer sequence
Lead with the specific researched detail about the prospect's company (from their site, LinkedIn, socials),
then connect it to the DC4 lens above only where it's a genuine fit; don't force the "Activity Doesn't Build
Pipeline" line into every email. Emails 2-3 should show DC4's thinking through story/insight/example, per the
voice guide's Story → Insight → Example → Takeaway structure, not through direct pitching.
