## What DC4 sells

B2B digital marketing agency services:
- Content strategy and creation (blogs, eBooks, audits)
- Search optimization (SEO and AI-focused AEO/answer-engine optimization)
- Paid media management (Google Ads, LinkedIn, Meta)
- Marketing automation (HubSpot implementation)
- Conversion rate optimization and landing pages
- White-label services for agencies

## Who they sell to

B2B marketing teams and marketing leaders trying to build pipeline and revenue growth, plus agencies who need a white-label partner.

## Core positioning

"Activity Doesn't Build Pipeline." DC4 positions itself against vanity-metric marketing (clicks, impressions, generic content calendars) and toward structured, results-driven programs that actually convert traffic into pipeline and revenue.

## Tone of voice

Direct and strategic, confident about their approach, plain language over jargon, professional but conversational. This pairs naturally with Jason Nuss's warmer, coach-like voice guide used for the actual email copy: DC4's positioning gives the substance, Jason's voice gives the delivery.

## Marketing approach specifics worth referencing in outreach

- Thought leadership content on emerging topics, particularly AI search/AEO.
- Client proof points emphasizing sustained, measurable growth (not one-off spikes).
- Educational content that demonstrates methodology rather than just claiming expertise.
- Emphasis on integration across channels (content + paid + automation + CRO) rather than siloed point solutions.
