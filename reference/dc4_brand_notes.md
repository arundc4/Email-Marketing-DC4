# DC4 (Digital C4) Brand Notes

> Bootstrapped from a live read of digitalc4.com since no `dc4_brand_notes.md`
> existed in this repo yet. Replace/expand with authored brand notes when available.

## What DC4 sells
B2B digital marketing services focused on building pipeline and revenue growth,
not just traffic or vanity metrics.

## Who they sell to
B2B marketing teams and leaders, especially those trying to optimize for both
traditional search and AI-generated answers (AEO). Also serves marketing
agencies via white-label partnerships.

## Core positioning
Headline message: **"Activity Doesn't Build Pipeline. We help B2B marketing
teams build the programs that do."**

Differentiation is results-driven outcomes over vanity metrics ("activity").

## Key phrases / brand voice cues
- "Clicks are easy to buy. Pipeline is harder to build."
- "Most teams have a platform. Few have a system."
- Strategic, no-nonsense, consulting-oriented voice.
- Challenges conventional thinking; positions DC4 as a solution architect,
  not a vendor.
- Direct, declarative language; emphasis on structural/systematic thinking.

## Services
- Content programs and blog management
- SEO / Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
- Paid media (Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta)
- Conversion rate optimization and landing page development
- HubSpot marketing automation
- White-label services for agencies

## How to use this in emails
When referencing DC4's approach in Email 3 of a sequence, tie it back to
"building a system, not just activity" and to whichever specific service
(content, AEO/SEO, paid media, CRO, HubSpot ops) most plausibly matches the
prospect's observed marketing challenges. Keep it a light mention, not a pitch.
