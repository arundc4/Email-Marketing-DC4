# DC4 Brand Notes

Source: compiled from public information on digitalc4.com (About Us page and search results) as of 2026-08-08. No internal brand document existed in the repo at time of writing; this file should be reviewed and corrected by DC4 if any detail below is inaccurate.

## Company
Digital C4 ("DC4"), founded 2010 by Jason Nuss and Chuck Swanson. 15+ years in business. Jason Nuss: founder/partner, 23+ years in B2B digital marketing.

## Positioning
A B2B digital marketing agency positioned as a strategic partner, not a vendor. Emphasizes senior-level involvement (founders stay engaged in client work) and accountability to business outcomes (pipeline, revenue) rather than vanity marketing metrics.

Tagline: "Digital C4 delivers more than marketing. We deliver momentum."

## Target customers
Mid-sized to enterprise B2B companies. Buyers are typically marketing directors, VPs of marketing, demand gen managers, and CMOs. Common verticals: SaaS, technology, education, professional services, though the approach generalizes to any B2B company needing expert execution without adding headcount.

## Services
- Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and traditional SEO
- Paid media (Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta)
- Content strategy and creation (blogs, eBooks, pillar pages)
- Marketing automation (HubSpot)
- Conversion rate optimization and landing page development
- White-label services for other agencies

## Approach / methodology
Acts as a team extension rather than an outside vendor. "Senior-level thinking on strategy, hands-on execution," with a hybrid onshore/offshore delivery model. Engagements are customized to the client's specific business goals rather than applying generic best-practice playbooks. Ties success to pipeline impact, not surface-level metrics like impressions or clicks.

## Differentiators to reference lightly in Email 3 (soft, no hard pitch)
- Founder-level involvement throughout the engagement, not handed off to junior staff
- Focus on pipeline/revenue outcomes over vanity metrics
- Custom strategy per client rather than templated playbooks
- Early, practical fluency in AI/answer-engine search visibility alongside classic SEO/paid channels
