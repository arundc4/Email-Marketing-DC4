# DC4 (Digital C4) Brand Notes

> Bootstrapped from public research (digitalc4.com, web search) because no brand notes
> file existed in this repo. This is a first-pass draft, not verified internal
> messaging. Recommend a DC4 stakeholder review and replace before this pipeline runs
> unattended on future batches.

## Company
- Digital C4 (DC4), founded 2010 by Jason Nuss and Chuck Swanson.
- Jason Nuss: founder & partner, 23+ years in B2B digital marketing.
- Phone: (503) 351-8995. Site: www.digitalc4.com.

## Positioning
- "B2B marketing strategy and execution. Built for pipeline."
- "Digital C4 delivers more than marketing. We deliver momentum."
- "Every engagement is built around your specific business goals, not generic best
  practices."
- Positions as a partner, not a vendor: senior-level thinking with hands-on execution,
  without adding headcount to internal teams.

## Tone of voice
Direct, results-focused, transparent. Accountability to business outcomes over vanity
metrics. Partnership and long-term involvement over transactional vendor relationships.

## Services
- Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
- SEO
- Paid Media (Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads)
- Content Strategy & Growth
- Marketing Automation (HubSpot, Pardot)
- Conversion Rate Optimization & Landing Pages
- White Label Services for Agencies

## Target audience
Mid-sized to enterprise B2B companies (SaaS, technology, cybersecurity, education,
professional services). Primary contacts: marketing directors, VPs, demand-gen
managers, CMOs who want expert execution without expanding internal headcount.

## Founder framing
Jason Nuss: "When an internal marketer looks good to their leadership team and the
company grows pipeline at the same time, that is the outcome he is building toward."

## How this should shape outreach
- Speak to the internal marketer's world (their goals, their leadership optics, their
  bandwidth), not just DC4's service menu.
- Lead with a specific, true observation about the prospect's company before anything
  about DC4.
- DC4 mentions should stay light until Email 3, and even then framed as an approach/
  example, not a pitch.
