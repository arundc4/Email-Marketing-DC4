# Digital C4 (DC4) Brand Notes

Source: researched live from www.digitalc4.com (homepage + about-us) and public search results,
since no dc4_brand_notes.md existed in the repo. Replace/expand with internal material if available.

## What DC4 is
Founded 2010 by Jason Nuss and Chuck Swanson. Portland, OR based. B2B marketing agency, 15+ years
in business. Jason Nuss: 23+ years in B2B digital marketing, founder/partner, personally involved
in client work (not a junior-staff handoff shop).

## Positioning
"B2B marketing strategy and execution. Built for pipeline."
Homepage headline: "Activity Doesn't Build Pipeline" / "We help B2B marketing teams build the
programs that do."
Tagline sentiment: "Digital C4 delivers more than marketing. We deliver momentum."

Core idea: they are a strategic partner extending an internal marketing team, not a vendor selling
tactics. Accountability to pipeline/revenue outcomes, not vanity metrics (impressions, traffic for
its own sake).

## Who they sell to
Mid-sized to enterprise B2B companies, especially SaaS, technology, cybersecurity, education, and
life sciences. Buyers are marketing directors, VPs of marketing, demand gen leaders, and CMOs who
need expert execution without adding full-time headcount.

## Services
- SEO and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO / AI-answer visibility)
- Paid media across Google, LinkedIn, and Meta, focused on audience quality and conversion intent
- Content programs aligned to visibility, authority, and revenue (not just volume)
- Marketing automation, especially HubSpot setup and lead management
- Conversion rate optimization / landing page optimization
- White-label execution for other agencies

## Proof points / clients
Client logos and testimonials include NWEA, Babel Street, Intellimize, Tektronix, Qorvo,
SlashNext, FormFactor. Testimonial highlights: Jean Fleming (NWEA) on a 5-year partnership and
audience growth; Eric Boyd (Babel Street) on "rapid, sustained growth in leads, conversions, and
pipeline."

## Voice/tone of the brand
Direct, strategic, outcomes-focused. Avoids hype and jargon. Leads with "clear thinking and good
ideas" over trendy tactics. Confident but not boastful; partnership language over vendor language.

## How to reference DC4 in outbound emails
- Keep it soft. One line, not a pitch. E.g. "This is the kind of gap we help B2B marketing teams
  close at DC4" rather than a services list.
- Anchor claims to things that are actually true above (pipeline focus, senior-level involvement,
  SEO/AEO, paid media, content, automation, CRO). Do not invent client results, numbers, or case
  studies that aren't sourced here.
- CTA language should stay low-pressure and conversational, matching "Start a Conversation" /
  "Let's Talk" rather than "Book a demo."
