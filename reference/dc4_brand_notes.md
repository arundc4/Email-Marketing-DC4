# DC4 (Digital C4) Brand Notes

Source: researched from digitalc4.com (homepage + about-us) and public web search, August 2026. No prior brand notes existed in this repo; these are grounded in DC4's own published positioning, not invented.

## Company

Digital C4 (also written D/C4, DC4) is a B2B digital marketing agency founded in 2010 by Jason Nuss and Chuck Swanson, headquartered in Portland, Oregon. Both founders remain hands-on in client work rather than handing everything to junior staff.

## Positioning

Homepage headline: "Activity Doesn't Build Pipeline. We help B2B marketing teams build the programs that do."

DC4 positions itself as a strategic partner, not a vendor executing isolated tactics. The core differentiator is accountability to pipeline and revenue impact, not vanity metrics like traffic or impressions.

## Services

- SEO and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization, i.e. visibility in AI-generated answers, not just traditional search)
- Paid media: Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads, focused on conversion quality over volume
- Content strategy: pillar pages, blog clusters, authority-building content programs tied to revenue goals
- Marketing automation: HubSpot implementation, landing page optimization, A/B testing
- White label services for other agencies

## Who they serve

Mid-sized to enterprise B2B companies, primarily SaaS, technology, cybersecurity, education, life sciences, and professional services. Buyers are typically marketing directors, VPs, demand gen managers, and CMOs who want expert execution without adding headcount.

## Brand voice cues (from the site itself)

Professional, direct, clarity-first. Client testimonials praise "clear thinking and good ideas." DC4 frames content and search structure as systems-level, architectural work, not just tactics. Tagline sentiment: "Digital C4 delivers more than marketing. We deliver momentum."

## How to use this in outreach

- Lead with the prospect's real, observable marketing setup (their tech stack, content, or positioning) before mentioning DC4 at all.
- When DC4's approach comes up (Email 3), keep it to one concrete, low-key example tied to the prospect's actual challenge (e.g. AEO/AI-search visibility, pipeline-vs-vanity-metrics, content-to-revenue alignment, marketing automation hygiene). Avoid generic agency claims.
- Never oversell. No em dashes, no hype words ("game-changing", "synergy", "best-in-class").
